# Arena

**Arena** is BlockchainRPG's player-versus-player (PvP) battle mode. Enter with a character, get matched with three other players, and fight in a **2v2 Skirmish**. Battles are automatic, so your character's preparation makes the difference.

**Play in:** [BlockchainRPG Dashboard → Arena](https://dash.blockchainrpg.io/arena). Arena is a **Dashboard** feature; you can continue using **Classic** for your other adventures with the same wallet, characters, and inventory.

## How to join a match

1. Open [Arena](https://dash.blockchainrpg.io/arena) in the Dashboard and connect your usual WAX wallet.
2. Choose an available character. Make sure it has enough health and the equipment you want to use.
3. Review the **1,000 staked GP entry fee** and confirm your entry.
4. Wait for a 2v2 match. Your character joins a team with another player and fights two opponents.
5. After the fight, check the result and your character's health, cooldown, and GP balance.

A match starts only when **four different players** have eligible entries waiting. Each player contributes **one character** to that match. The queue prioritizes entries that have waited longest; it does **not** currently match players based on character level or strength. Your own characters will not be matched against one another.

### Enter with multiple characters

You can select **up to 20 available characters** and join the queue in one action. Each character is a **separate entry**, not part of a premade team. Check the total entry cost before confirming.

## Entry fees, prizes, and refunds

Every match has four entries, each costing **1,000 staked GP**.

| Outcome | What happens to your 1,000 GP entry |
| --- | --- |
| **Win** | Receive **1,800 GP** in your staked balance: your entry plus **800 GP net winnings** |
| **Lose** | Receive no GP payout; your **1,000 GP entry is lost** |
| **Cancel while waiting** | Receive a **full 1,000 GP refund** |

The four entries make a **4,000 GP pot**. Of that, **400 GP (10%) is burned**, and the remaining **3,600 GP** is split between the two winning players.

:::note
Waiting entries **do not automatically expire**. You can cancel only **before your entry begins matching**. A fight that is already being matched cannot be canceled. Refunds and payouts may require time to confirm. You can cancel several eligible waiting entries together from the Arena page.
:::

## What happens to my character?

Your character's **attack, defense, and current health** are recorded when it enters the queue. Once queued, the character is busy: it cannot be used for other activities or have its gear changed until it leaves the queue or finishes its match.

**Arena damage carries over** after the fight. Your character also receives a cooldown:

- **Survived:** 5 minutes.
- **Knocked out:** 60 minutes.

Before joining, make sure your fighter has enough HP and the artifacts you want equipped. Afterward, check its health and cooldown before using it again.

## Watch replays and track results

Open a completed match from your **Arena history** to see both teams, replay the duels, or jump to the result. You can play, pause, step through duels, and expand the battle log to see what happened.

Arena history also shows your **wins, losses, win rate, and GP results**. Select a player's wallet name to open their profile.

## Daily Arena quest

Complete **one Arena fight**, win or lose, to earn **one Rabbit Stew** from the daily quest.

1. Finish a match and allow its result to confirm.
2. Open [Dashboard → Quests](https://dash.blockchainrpg.io/quests).
3. Claim your Rabbit Stew when the quest is ready.

Daily quests reset at **midnight UTC**. Your fight counts for the day its **results are confirmed**, which may differ from the day you joined the queue.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why am I still waiting for a match?

Arena needs **four different players** to have eligible entries in the queue. Several characters from the same wallet do not fill multiple places in one match. Your waiting entry does not expire on its own; you can cancel it for a full refund before matching begins.

### Can I choose my teammate?

No. Teams are assigned automatically. Selecting several characters does not create a premade team.

### Will I face players with similar-level characters?

Not necessarily. The initial 2v2 mode uses queue waiting time rather than character strength or level for matchmaking.

### Can my characters fight each other?

No. Your own characters will not be placed on opposing teams.

### Can I cancel an Arena entry?

Yes, **while it is still waiting**. You'll get the full entry fee back. Once matching begins, cancellation is unavailable.

### Can I hunt, heal, or change equipment while queued?

No. A queued character is busy until its entry is canceled or the match is resolved. Prepare its health and equipment **before** joining.

### Is Arena available in Classic?

The Arena interface is available in **Dashboard**, not Classic. Your game account and progress are shared, so you can open Dashboard with the same wallet to participate.

### Where can I see whether I won?

Open **Arena → History** in the Dashboard to view completed matches, results, and replays. Balance and character updates may take time to confirm.
