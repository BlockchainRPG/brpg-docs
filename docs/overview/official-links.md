# Official Links

The blockchain gaming space unfortunately attracts scammers and impersonators who create fake websites, fake "support" accounts, and malicious links designed to drain your wallet. **This page is the single source of truth for our official channels.** If a link, account, or website isn't listed here, treat it with suspicion.

:::caution Stay safe
We will **never** DM you first asking for your seed phrase, private keys, or wallet password, and we will never ask you to "validate" your wallet on a third-party site. Always double-check the domain before connecting your wallet, and when in doubt, ask in our official [Discord](https://discord.blockchainrpg.io) before clicking anything.
:::

Scammers often mint fake items under look-alike collection names. Always confirm assets belong to the **`brpg`** (or legacy **`blokchainrpg`**) collection before buying on the secondary market.
:::

## How to Spot a Fake

A few quick habits will keep your account and assets safe:

- **Check the domain carefully.** Official sites end in `blockchainrpg.io`. Watch for look-alikes with extra words, hyphens, or different endings.
- **Be skeptical of unsolicited DMs.** Our staff won't message you first offering "support," airdrops, or giveaways that require connecting your wallet.
- **Never share secrets.** No legitimate part of the game ever needs your seed phrase or private keys.
- **When unsure, ask first.** Post in our official Discord before acting on any link or offer.

If you ever spot a scam or impersonator, please report it in our [Discord](https://discord.blockchainrpg.io) so we can warn other players and take action. For anything else, see our [Contact page](/contact).
