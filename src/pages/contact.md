# Contact Us

We're a community-driven project, and staying in touch with our players is a core part of how we build the game. Whether you need help, want to report a bug, have a partnership proposal, or just want to say hello, here's how to reach us.

## Player Support

For account help, gameplay questions, bug reports, or anything else game-related, the best ways to reach us are:

- **Discord (fastest):** Our [official Discord server](https://discord.blockchainrpg.io) is the most active place to get help. Our team and community are there daily, and most questions are answered quickly. Open a support ticket in the dedicated channel for account-specific issues.
- **Email:** You can reach our support team at **support@blockchainrpg.io**. Email is best for issues that involve private account details you'd rather not share publicly.

When contacting support about an account issue, it helps to include your **WAX wallet address** and a clear description of the problem (and a screenshot, if relevant) so we can assist you as quickly as possible. Please never share your private keys, seed phrase, or wallet password with anyone — our team will never ask for them.
